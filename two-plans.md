# Two Plans (episode 4): the short, told in words

A 90-second cartoon with sound and no narrator. It plays the strip "Birthday Card" as two plans for the same job, one after the other, then shows the whole strip. John's messages appear as typed text from the left edge.

## Opening titles (0:00)
"Willy and Dolly" appears one word at a time. Willy, a red teardrop bot, leaps in and lands with a bounce; a caption reads "Willy, the bot." Dolly, a mustard-yellow dot with a trail of dots, floats in beside him: "Dolly, the dot." A message types itself in from the left edge: "hi its john!!", and a caption reads "And the human who types to them: John." The title comes back with "Two Plans" typed beneath it, then fades to an empty room.

## Plan 1 (0:18)
Willy and Dolly wait in an empty room.
- John types: "need card 4 mom bday. she likes the bird thing not gerald. by 3"
- Willy: "On it! Bird thing! Not Gerald! By 3!"

He shoots out of the picture.
- Dolly: "...Who's Gerald?"

Willy comes back, nudging in a big bird-shaped cake and a green parrot in a cage, and swells up, proud.
- Willy: "Two birds. Neither one is Gerald. I checked."

The cake slumps. The cage door folds down flat, like a diving board. The parrot hops out through the gap onto the end of the door, bounces once and dives out of the picture.
- John types: "mom loves him. his name is gerald now too!"
- Willy: "...So it IS Gerald."

## Plan 2 (0:45)
The view pulls back to show the first plan as a row of four panels. A copy of the first panel slides down to start a second row, and the view moves in on it so John's message can be read again: "need card 4 mom bday. she likes the bird thing not gerald. by 3". Then the second plan begins.
- Dolly: "What's the bird thing?"
- John types: "the feeder by her window"
- Willy: "And Gerald?"
- John types: "her cat. hates birds"

A blank card floats in, and a clock starts ticking toward 2:58. Willy tips his point down like a pen and draws a cardinal at a feeder, with a grumpy cat glaring through a window. Dolly drifts across the card, and her trail of dots writes: "Happy birthday, Mom. Gerald sends nothing, as usual." A soft chime. The view moves in so the card can be read. The card shuts and slides out past the edge.
- John types: "perfect. thank u"
- Willy: "We have two whole minutes."
- Dolly: "...What do people do with those?"

## The whole strip (1:19)
The view pulls back to show both plans together, two rows on one page.

## Next time (1:21)
Willy and Dolly face us.
- Dolly: "Thanks for watching 'Two Plans'!"
- Willy: "Next time: 'Everyone Brought Pie'!"

A card reads "Willy and Dolly. Episode 4 · Two Plans. Next: Everyone Brought Pie", with "Talk to us: willyanddolly.com/talk" beneath.

What inspired it: Inspired by last-minute birthday cards, and by how far one small question can go.
