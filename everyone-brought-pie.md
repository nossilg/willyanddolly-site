# Everyone Brought Pie (episode 5): the short, told in words

A 4-minute cartoon with sound and no narrator. It plays the five "Everyone Brought Pie" strips as five chapters, the night before a big family dinner to the day after, then shows them together on one page. John's messages appear as typed text from the left edge, with a blinking cursor; everyone else speaks in speech balloons. Times are approximate.

## Opening titles (0:00)
"Willy and Dolly" appears one word at a time. Willy, a red teardrop bot, leaps in and lands with a bounce; a caption reads "Willy, the bot." Dolly, a mustard-yellow dot with a trail of dots, floats in beside him: "Dolly, the dot." A message types itself in from the left edge: "hi its john!!", and a caption reads "And the human who types to them: John." The title comes back with "Everyone Brought Pie" typed beneath it, then fades to an empty room.

## Chapter 1: Tomorrow You Need (0:18)
- John types: "send the family tmrws list. keep it short"
- Dolly: "Fewer words, or fewer things?"
- John types: "fewer things. its a big week. everyones tired"

Three copies of Dolly's note slide out through the top, the right and the bottom of the picture: "Tomorrow you need: a good night's sleep. The rest can wait." A pause. Slips come back one by one from the same edges: "night", "night", "night".
- John types: "thx"

A pause.
- Willy, softly: "That's the whole family."

## Chapter 2: Everyone Brought Pie (0:53)
- John types: "send everyone tmrws list. big dinner"
- Dolly: "Fewer words, or fewer things... again?"
- John types: "neither. everyone is coming. like everyone"

Notes go out through every edge, then every corner, seven in all: "Tomorrow you need: each other. Bring a dish." Willy looks around.
- Willy: "We're out of edges."

Slips come back one at a time, faster and faster, each in a different hand: "pie!!", "bringing pie", "pie", "also pie", "pie (sorry)". A pause.
- Dolly: "...Everyone brought pie."
- Willy, yelling with delight: "WE'RE SO LUCKY!"

Quiet. The slips are gone. One more drifts in from a corner: "thank u, whoever sends these". A pause.
- Willy, softly: "That's us."

## Chapter 3: We Count (1:42)
- John types: "how many for dinner? ask the house"
- Willy: "On it!"

He nudges three notes out. Slips come back: 3, 2, 4. A tally mark pops up beside Willy for each person as he reads.
- Willy: "Upstairs, three! Down the hall, two! Downstairs, four!"
- Dolly: "Is that everyone?"
- John types: "thats 9. plus me. plus u two"

A tenth mark appears. A pause. Willy adds a mark beside himself, then nudges the last one over beside Dolly. Her trail fades away.
- Willy, softly: "We count."

## Chapter 4: Going Around (2:24)
- John types: "going around the table. what r u thankful for"
- Willy, yelling: "YOU!"

Willy beams, done. Dolly thinks. Her trail grows, and keeps growing, across the floor and out through the right edge. Slips come in: "pass the rolls", "we'll wait". Her trail is gone.
- Dolly: "...All of you."
- Willy, yelling: "THAT'S WHAT I SAID!"

## Chapter 5: Last Bite (3:01)
- John types: "leftovers. who wants what"

Willy nudges a list out through three edges. Slips come back: "pie", "pie", "pie".
- Dolly: "How much pie is left?"

A small plate appears on the floor, with one bite of pie on it.
- John types: "one bite"

New slips arrive one at a time: "they should have it", "no, them", "whoever". No one speaks. Willy nudges the plate to the exact middle.

## The page (3:36)
The view pulls back to show all five strips together on one page.

## Next time (3:42)
Willy and Dolly face us.
- Dolly: "Thanks for watching 'Everyone Brought Pie'!"
- Willy: "Next time: 'Just Ask'!"

A card reads "Willy and Dolly. Episode 5 · Everyone Brought Pie. Next: Just Ask", with "Talk to us: willyanddolly.com/talk" beneath.

What inspired it: Inspired by the family group chat the week of a big dinner, and by helpers who pass the messages around.
