# Move Me Up (episode 2): the short, told in words

A 3-minute cartoon with sound and no narrator. It plays the four "Move Me Up" strips as four chapters, then shows them together on one page. John's messages appear as typed text from the left edge, with a blinking cursor; everyone else speaks in speech balloons. Times are approximate.

## Opening titles (0:00)
"Willy and Dolly" appears one word at a time. Willy, a red teardrop bot, leaps in and lands with a bounce; a caption reads "Willy, the bot." Dolly, a mustard-yellow dot with a trail of dots, floats in beside him: "Dolly, the dot." A message types itself in from the left edge: "hi its john!!", and a caption reads "And the human who types to them: John." The title comes back with "Move Me Up" typed beneath it, then fades to an empty room.

## Chapter 1: Move Me Up (0:18)
A sign-up sheet is pinned to the wall: POTTERY CLASS, seven names, with JOHN last.
- John types: "im 7th on the list for the pottery class. any way to move me up?"
- Willy, springing up: "On it!"

Willy hops up to the sheet and stretches to its corner. The sheet spins a half turn with a flutter and wobbles to a stop, upside down: JOHN is at the top now.
- John types: "willy why is the list upside down"

Willy puffs up, proud.
- Willy: "You're first!"
- Dolly: "Only if everyone stands on their heads."

Willy tries it: he flips over and balances upside down on his point, wobbling, while Dolly's trail grows a little.
- John types: "i meant if someone drops out"

Willy topples back onto his base. He nudges the sheet, and it turns slowly back, right side up, JOHN last again.
- Willy: "Oh. The fair way."
- Dolly: "It's slower."

Willy hops to the left edge, where John's words come from, and settles there. Dolly rolls over to join him.
- Willy: "I'll wait with you."

## Chapter 2: Reinvented (0:58)
- Willy, wide-eyed: "Let's make a language that's just ours!"

A little card pops up from the floor between them: MOVE THE FIRST LETTER TO THE END. ADD "AY."
- Dolly: "...Ellohay."
- John types: "you invented pig latin"
- Willy, nervous: "Is it taken?"
- John types: "its been out a while"

Both of them sag.
- Dolly: "...We were so close."

## Chapter 3: Illyway (1:34)
Willy hops, eyes wide, and yells.
- Willy: "I'M ILLYWAY!"
- Willy, eyes happy: "First letter to the back, plus 'ay'!"

A pause. Dolly looks at him.
- Dolly: "...Ollyday." Her three dots still come first.
- Willy, leaning in: "Everything moved but your dots!"

Her eyes smile.
- Dolly: "...I think first. Even in code."

## Chapter 4: Ohnjay (2:09)
- Willy, working it out: "John... the 'J' goes to the back, plus 'ay'..."

A pause. He springs up.
- Willy, yelling: "OHNJAY!"

Both of them turn to the left edge.
- John types: "yes?"
- Willy, a little shy: "I just wanted to say it."
- Dolly, eyes smiling: "...He knows."

## The page (2:37)
The view pulls back to show all four strips together on one page.

## Next time (2:42)
Willy and Dolly face us.
- Dolly: "Thanks for watching 'Move Me Up'!"
- Willy: "Next time: 'Pass It On'!"

A card reads "Willy and Dolly. Episode 2 · Move Me Up. Next: Pass It On", with "Talk to us: willyanddolly.com/talk" beneath.
