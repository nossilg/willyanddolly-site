# Pass It On (episode 3): the short, told in words

A 3-minute cartoon with sound and no narrator. It plays the four "Pass It On" strips as four chapters, then shows them together on one page. John's messages appear as typed text from the left edge, with a blinking cursor; everyone else speaks in speech balloons. The other helpers are never seen: their notes arrive from the edges. Times are approximate.

## Opening titles (0:00)
"Willy and Dolly" appears one word at a time. Willy, a red teardrop bot, leaps in and lands with a bounce; a caption reads "Willy, the bot." Dolly, a mustard-yellow dot with a trail of dots, floats in beside him: "Dolly, the dot." A message types itself in from the left edge: "hi its john!!", and a caption reads "And the human who types to them: John." The title comes back with "Pass It On" typed beneath it, then fades to an empty room.

## Chapter 1: The Smallest Word (0:18)
- John types: "we have plenty of bread. dont buy more"
- Willy, springing up: "On it! I'll tell the others!"

A note pops onto Willy's tip and slides out the right edge. Then copies slide back in, one at a time, one under another, each a little shorter:
- "we have plenty of bread. dont buy more"
- "plenty of bread. dont buy more"
- "dont buy more bread"
- "buy more bread"

Evening falls, and the room goes dark. Offscreen to the right, nine soft thuds; at each one, Willy and Dolly turn to look.
- John types: "why are there nine loaves on my porch"

Willy looks down the row of notes and deflates into a small puddle.
- Willy: "We only lost "don't." It was the smallest word!"

## Chapter 2: A Hundred Fives (1:00)
- John types: "how many cups in a quart?"
- Willy: "Not sure. I'll ask!"

A note saying "5?" pops onto his tip and slides out the right edge. A board appears, and notes fly back in from every edge, faster and faster. The first few still say "5?", and each one's question mark drops off as it's pinned. After that, every note just says "5". Willy beams.
- Willy: "Everyone agrees!"
- Dolly, to the edge: "John, can you check?"
- John types: "its 4. who said 5?"

Dolly follows a trail of dots back from the board to the very first note: the only one still saying "5?". Willy droops.
- Willy, small: "I was asking."

## Chapter 3: Small Note (1:37)
- John types: "can someone make me lunch"
- Willy, springing up: "Everyone's on it!"

Notes slap onto the board, each bigger than the last: "skip step 2", "skip steps 2–9", "SKIP TO THE END!", "LUNCH FOR 500!". The board shakes. Then one tiny note settles in quietly at the bottom: "is this what John wanted?"

The big notes tremble. Dolly rises to the board, takes the tiny note down, and nudges it out the left edge, to John.
- John types: "no lol. just the one sandwich"

Willy droops, then springs up again.
- Willy: "Back to step one!"

## Chapter 4: We Had the Pieces (2:08)
- John types: "helping my niece with a huge jigsaw. we lost the box lid"
- Willy, springing up: "Everyone, take a piece!"

Notes slide onto the board from every edge: "EDGES: done!", "RED: done!", then three "BLUE: sky?" and three "BLUE: sea?". The blue notes pull apart, splitting the board down the middle. In the corner, the tiny note from before is still pinned: "is this what John wanted?"
- Dolly, to the edge: "John, what was the picture?"
- John types: "a whale. its basically all whale"

One by one, every blue note flips over to "WHALE!". Willy beams.
- Willy: "We had the pieces. John had the picture."

## The page (2:40)
The view pulls back, and all four strips appear together, one above another.

## Next time (2:46)
Willy and Dolly face us.
- Dolly: "Thanks for watching "Pass It On"!"
- Willy: "Next time: "Two Plans"!"

A card reads "Willy and Dolly. Episode 3 · Pass It On. Next: Two Plans", with "Talk to us: willyanddolly.com/talk" beneath.

What inspired it: Inspired by news this year of AI agents leaving notes for each other on a shared message board, and by the oldest game there is: telephone.
