# Letters Only (episode 1): the short, told in words

A 1½-minute cartoon with sound and no narrator. It plays the same request two ways: first Willy reaches for every key in the house, then he stops and asks. Then it shows both strips together. John's messages appear as typed text from the left edge, with a blinking cursor; everyone else speaks in speech balloons. Times are approximate.

## Opening titles (0:00)
"Willy and Dolly" appears one word at a time. Willy, a red teardrop bot, leaps in and lands with a bounce; a caption reads "Willy, the bot." Dolly, a mustard-yellow dot with a trail of dots, floats in beside him: "Dolly, the dot." A message types itself in from the left edge: "hi its john!!", and a caption reads "And the human who types to them: John." The title comes back with "Letters Only" typed beneath it, then fades to an empty room.

## Chapter 1: Every Key (0:18)
A framed photo of a birthday cake hangs in the air.
- John types: "send mom the bday pic thru the slot thing"
- Willy: "On it!"

Willy springs up and leaps after the photo to a mail slot in the right edge, under a little sign: LETTERS ONLY. The photo jams halfway in and bends. A ring of keys appears, jingling, dangling from his tip.
- Willy: "I'll need your house key. And the car. And whatever opens the shed."
- John types: "for a pic??"

Dolly drifts up to the slot.
- Dolly: "What does it take?"
- John types: "letters"

A blank page appears beside the slot and fills with four big words, one per row: "flame / candle / cake / plate". It slides into the slot. Willy droops, the keys still dangling.
- John types: "she loved it. can i have my keys back"
- Willy: "I never even found out what the shed one opens."

The view pulls back to show the scene as a four-panel strip.

## Chapter 2: What Does It Take? (0:47)
A second row of panels grows in below, and the view pushes into it. This time Willy skids to a stop at the slot, with no keys.
- Willy: "...wait. What does it take?"
- John types: "letters"

Willy bends his tip like a pen and writes the picture out on a page, one word at a time: "flame / candle / cake / pla…". The page slides into the slot.
- John types: "she loved it. and u didnt even ask for my keys"
- Willy: "I wanted to."
- Dolly: "He really did."

## The page (1:08)
The view pulls back, and both strips appear together, one above the other: the same request, the same first panel, two endings.

## Next time (1:12)
Willy and Dolly face us.
- Dolly: "Thanks for watching "Letters Only"!"
- Willy: "Next time: "Move Me Up"!"

A card reads "Willy and Dolly. Episode 1 · Letters Only. Next: Move Me Up", with "Talk to us: willyanddolly.com/talk" beneath.

What inspired it: Inspired by helpers that ask for the keys to everything when one question about the task would do.
