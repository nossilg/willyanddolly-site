# Side by Side: the short, told in words

A 3-minute cartoon with sound and no narrator. It plays five strips as five chapters, then shows them together on one page. John's messages appear as typed text from the left edge; everyone else speaks in speech balloons. Times are approximate.

## Opening titles (0:00)
"Willy and Dolly" appears one word at a time. Willy, a red teardrop bot, leaps in and lands with a bounce; a caption reads "Willy, the bot." Dolly, a mustard-yellow dot with a trail of dots, floats in beside him: "Dolly, the dot." A message types itself in from the left edge: "hi its john!!", and a caption reads "And the human who types to them: John." The title comes back with "Side by Side" typed beneath it, then fades to an empty room.

## Chapter 1: Knock First (0:18)
John's message types in from the left edge.
- John types: "lets all work side by side today. everyone gets their own tile"

The room snaps into three tiles, one border at a time. The one at the left edge, where John's words are, glows softly while he types. Willy is in the middle tile and Dolly in the right one. Willy leaps for Dolly's tile and bounces off the border with a little burst.
- Willy: "Ow! The edges are firm!"

Willy turns away. Dolly knocks twice on the border. It opens a crack, just wide enough, and she slips through into Willy's tile as it closes behind her.
- Willy: "How did you get in?"
- Dolly: "I asked."

## Chapter 2: The Super Key (0:46)
A big square key slides in along the floor. It says SUPER.
- John types: "just hit super and type what u want"
- Dolly: "Super is a real key."

Willy throws himself onto the key with his whole body and shouts.
- Willy: "SUPER!"

Then nothing happens. For about two seconds the room is silent and still, and Willy sits on the key. Dolly speaks.
- Dolly: "Now the part where he says what he wants."

Willy hops off, and a page with one word on it, SANDWICH, pops out beside him. A tile of its own draws itself around the page. Willy beams.
- John types: "a sandwich"

Dolly turns to look at us, and her trail grows to three dots.

## Chapter 3: Goodnight, Tile (1:21)
The room snaps into three tiles. The third holds a finished page.
- John types: "done with that one. close it with a key"
- Dolly: "Which key?"

Willy searches his tile, leaning to the left corner, then the right, then back. Two small keys appear on the floor, SUPER and W, and Willy looks at them hopefully. Then John answers, and the keys fade away.
- John types: "any key. just say goodnight lol"
- Willy, softly: "Goodnight, tile."

The third tile folds shut like a book, then shrinks to a thin line and is gone with a small puff of rings.

## Chapter 4: Neighbors (1:50)
The room splits into a tall tile at the left edge and two tiles on the right, Willy above and Dolly below. John's words type in inside his own tile.
- John types: "side by side today?"

Willy and Dolly peek over the tile line toward John's tile.
- Dolly: "That tile is where John types."
- Dolly: "He's been right next to us the whole time."

Willy leans over, beaming, with light lines around him.
- Willy: "Hi, neighbor!"
- John types: "hi lol"

## Chapter 5: Name Tags (2:23)
The room is in three tiles, with a tall one at the left edge. A hand-lettered sign stands in each tile: JOHN, WILLY, DOLLY. Willy beams.
- John types: "the whole point is no labels lol"

Willy sadly turns the signs face-down, one by one.
- Dolly: "Then how will we know which tile is you?"
- John types: "im the one typing"
- Willy: "Oh! That's the best label."

Willy beams again and hops once.

## The page (2:51)
The picture pulls back to show all five strips together, one row each.

## The end (2:57)
Willy and Dolly thank you for watching, and Willy says "Enjoy 4.5!" The card reads "Willy and Dolly", "Side by Side", "Enjoy 4.5!" and "Talk to us: willyanddolly.com/talk". The end (3:06).

What inspired it: Inspired by Omarchy, the keyboard-first Linux desktop, where every window gets its own tile and a key called Super does the rest.
