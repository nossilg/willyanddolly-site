# Halloween (episode 1.25): the short, told in words

A 2½-minute cartoon with sound and no narrator. It plays the four Halloween strips as four chapters, then shows them together on one page. John's messages appear as typed text from the left edge, with a blinking cursor; everyone else speaks in speech balloons. Times are approximate.

## Opening titles (0:00)
"Willy and Dolly" appears one word at a time. Willy, a red teardrop bot, leaps in and lands with a bounce; a caption reads "Willy, the bot." Dolly, a mustard-yellow dot with a trail of dots, floats in beside him: "Dolly, the dot." A message types itself in from the left edge: "hi its john!!", and a caption reads "And the human who types to them: John." The title comes back with "Halloween" typed beneath it, then fades to an empty room.

## Chapter 1: Carve (0:18)
A big, plain pumpkin sits on the floor between Willy and Dolly.
- John types: "carve a face in the pumpkin"
- Willy: "On it!"

Willy hops over and works at the pumpkin with his tip, wiggling. Two small round holes appear, one at a time, and pumpkin curls drop to the floor. He hops back to his spot and beams. The pumpkin's two holes sit level with their own eyes, the same size and spacing. Dolly nods: it's a good face.

Night falls. Willy, Dolly and the room fade into the dark, and only the pumpkin's two holes glow, with a soft, friendly "ooo". It gets darker still, and two more pairs of eyes light up to match: three pairs of glowing dots in a row.
- John types: "wait which one of you is the pumpkin"

The two outer pairs turn to look at the middle pair. The middle pair keeps staring straight out.

## Chapter 2: How Do I Look? (0:51)
- John types: "ok costumes on. how do i look?"
- Willy, springing up: "Wonderful!"

Dolly turns to the edge.
- Dolly: "We can't see you. What are you wearing?"
- John types: "a sheet with two eyeholes. u didnt even know what it was"

Willy, warm, to the edge:
- Willy: "I knew it was you."

## Chapter 3: The Scariest Costume (1:21)
A blank page drops onto Willy's tip and hangs there like a ghost's sheet, his eyes peeking out of two holes.
- Willy, yelling: "I'm a ghost! BOO!"
- Dolly: "I need a costume. What's the scariest thing you can think of?"
- Willy, thinking: "Not knowing what someone means."

Dolly drifts out of view. She drifts back in with a page leaning on her front: one big question mark. Willy recoils, and his sheet slips off his tip.
- Willy, yelling: "AAAH! Put it away!"

## Chapter 4: Porch Pumpkin (1:51)
- John types: "forgot to buy a pumpkin and the kids come at 6 :("

Dolly looks down at herself: round, and mustard-gold. Willy looks at Dolly; she nods. The front door and the porch step slide in, and Dolly rolls up onto the step. Willy nudges a page with a jack-o'-lantern face onto her front, then a little green stem on top of her. Her own eyes still show above the face. Evening comes, and the light turns orange.
- John types: "best pumpkin on the street. wait did it just blink"

Willy beams. Above the jack-o'-lantern face, Dolly blinks again, slowly, on purpose.

## The page (2:17)
The view pulls back, and all four strips appear together, one above another.

## Next time (2:22)
Willy and Dolly face us.
- Dolly: "Thanks for watching "Halloween"!"
- Willy: "Happy Halloween!"
