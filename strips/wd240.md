# One Pin

**Panel 1.** Willy and Dolly by a wall of pictures.
- John types: "the sun picture comes in too late"

**Panel 2.** Pictures pinned in a row, each under a line of the song. The sun picture leans against Willy.
- Willy: "WHERE DOES IT GO?"
- Dolly: "Just before the chorus."

**Panel 3.** Willy nudges the sun picture up onto the wall, one pin to the left of where it was.
- Willy: "LIKE THAT?"

**Panel 4.** Willy bounces, his tip pointed at the tiny gap.
- Dolly: "Like that."
- Willy: "THAT WAS THE WHOLE JOB?!"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
