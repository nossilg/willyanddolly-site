# Off the Clock

**Panel 1.** Seven helpers sit at a counter behind mugs with their names on them. Willy and Dolly are in the middle. A list on the wall: build, debug, retry, retry, all checked.

**Panel 2.** "Rest" gets its check. Everyone but Dolly dozes off.

**Panel 3.**
- Dolly: "Same time tomorrow?"
- Willy, eyes shut: "Same time tomorrow."

**Panel 4.** A big box asks "One more...?" with NO and YES. The pointer is on YES. Everyone is wide awake, and Willy tilts away, whistling.

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
