# Tomorrow You Need

**Panel 1.** Willy on the left, Dolly on the right.
- John types: "send the family tmrws list. keep it short"
- Dolly: "Fewer words, or fewer things?"

**Panel 2.**
- John types: "fewer things. its a big week. everyones tired"

**Panel 3.** Both of them beam as three copies of Dolly's note leave through the top, the right and the bottom of the panel. Each reads: "Tomorrow you need: a good night's sleep. The rest can wait."

**Panel 4.** Small slips come back from the same three edges, each in a neat hand: "night", "night", "night".
- John types: "thx"
- Willy, softly: "That's the whole family."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
