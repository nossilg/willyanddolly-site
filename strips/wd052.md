# The Super Key

**Panel 1.** A big square key sits on the floor between Willy and Dolly. It has one word on it: SUPER. A box at the top left holds John's message.
- John types: "just hit super and type what u want"
- Dolly, to us: "Super is a real key."

**Panel 2.** Willy has thrown himself onto the key, eyes wide and mouth open, with short lines around him.
- Willy, in a jagged shout balloon: "SUPER!"

**Panel 3.** Nothing has happened. Willy sits on the key with a flat, unimpressed look. Dolly smiles with closed eyes.
- Dolly: "Now the part where he says what he wants."

**Panel 4.** Willy tilts to one side beside the key and holds out a page with one word on it: SANDWICH. He looks pleased, with small lines around him.
- John types: "a sandwich"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
