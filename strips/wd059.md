# So Sure, strip 5 of 5: "Which Robots"

**Panel 1.** Willy and Dolly turn toward the edge. Three little paper slips, in another person's handwriting, come in from the right and the top: John's coworkers.
- John types: "everyone at work started smoking"
- A coworker: "the robots will fix it later"
- Another: "robots'll fix it"
- Another: "later, the robots"

**Panel 2.** No words. Willy and Dolly look around the room for the robots: Willy over his shoulder, Dolly lifting up to look higher.

**Panel 3.** No words. They look at each other.

**Panel 4.** Beside Willy, a half-folded blue sock. Willy looks down at it.
- Willy, small: "We're still learning to fold socks."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
