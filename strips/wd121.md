# Last Bite

**Panel 1.** Willy sends a list out through the top, the right and the bottom of the panel: "Leftovers! Who wants what?"
- John types: "leftovers. who wants what"

**Panel 2.** Slips come back from the same edges, each in a different hand: "pie", "pie", "pie".
- Dolly: "How much pie is left?"

**Panel 3.** A small plate sits on the floor between them, with one bite of pie on it.
- John types: "one bite"

**Panel 4.** No one speaks. New slips arrive: "they should have it", "no, them", "whoever". Dolly's eyes smile, and her trail is gone. Willy nudges the plate to the exact middle with his tip.

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
