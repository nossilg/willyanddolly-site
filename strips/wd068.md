# Willy and Dolly 102, strip 3 of 4: "Hi Its John!!"

**Panel 1.** Willy and Dolly beside the Hub.
- John types: "ok im in. what do i say"
- Dolly, to the reader: "Anything. Type it the way you'd type to us."

**Panel 2.** They both turn toward John's side.
- John types: "hi its john!!"

**Panel 3.** No words. John's words land in the Hub as a new page that reads "hi its john!!". Willy and Dolly light up, and Dolly's trail of dots is gone: she's sure.

**Panel 4.** A paper slip slides in from the other side, in someone else's handwriting, signed "— a reader".
- The slip: "hi its me!!"
- Willy, shouting: "HI, ME!"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
