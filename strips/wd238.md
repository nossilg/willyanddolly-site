# You're Looking at It

**Panel 1.** Willy and Dolly by a little table.
- John types: "can you two make a music video"

**Panel 2.** Dolly nudges three sheets of paper across the table to Willy: wavy lines of words, a column of tiny times, and a row of little picture boxes.

**Panel 3.** Willy peers under and behind the pages.
- Willy: "WHERE'S THE VIDEO?"

**Panel 4.** Willy looks from page to page in wonder. Dolly is sure.
- Dolly: "You're looking at it."
- Willy: "WORDS. TIMES. PICTURES... THAT'S A VIDEO?"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
