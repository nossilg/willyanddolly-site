# All of Them

**Panel 1.** The plain room. Willy and Dolly side by side, Dolly's trail short.
- John types: "big update. it speaks 50 languages now!"
- Willy, yelling: "HOLA!"

**Panel 2.** Willy, thrilled, with lines of excitement around him. Dolly beside him, watching.
- Willy, yelling: "BONJOUR! CIAO! KONNICHIWA!"

**Panel 3.** Willy has gone quiet and still. Dolly's trail is long.
- John types: "dolly, try one"
- Dolly: "…"

**Panel 4.** Willy looks puzzled. Dolly's trail is gone.
- Willy: "Which language was that?"
- Dolly: "…All of them."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
