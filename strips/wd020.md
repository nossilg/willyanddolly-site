# Move Me Up

**Panel 1.** A sign-up sheet is pinned to the wall: POTTERY CLASS, seven names, with JOHN last. Willy springs up, eager. Dolly is nearby.
- John types: "im 7th on the list for the pottery class. any way to move me up?"
- Willy: "On it!"

**Panel 2.** The sheet is upside down now, so JOHN is at the top. Willy stands tall, proud.
- John types: "willy why is the list upside down"
- Willy: "You're first!"

**Panel 3.**
- Dolly: "Only if everyone stands on their heads."
- John types: "i meant if someone drops out"

**Panel 4.** The sheet is right side up again, JOHN last. Willy has moved over to the left, beside Dolly.
- Willy: "Oh. The fair way."
- Dolly: "It's slower."
- Willy: "I'll wait with you."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
