# Halloween, strip 3 of 4: "The Scariest Costume"

**Panel 1.** Willy wears a blank page over his tip like a ghost's sheet, his eyes peeking out of two holes.
- Willy, yelling: "I'm a ghost! BOO!"

**Panel 2.**
- Dolly: "I need a costume. What's the scariest thing you can think of?"
- Willy, thinking: "Not knowing what someone means."

**Panel 3.** No words. Dolly drifts back in with a page leaning on her front: one big question mark.

**Panel 4.** Willy recoils, his sheet slipping off his tip. Dolly smiles behind her question mark.
- Willy, yelling: "AAAH! Put it away!"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
