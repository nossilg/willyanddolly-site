# Knock First

**Panel 1.** The panel is split into three neat square tiles along the floor: an empty one at the left edge, then Willy's, then Dolly's. Willy and Dolly both smile with closed eyes. A box at the top left holds John's message.
- John types: "lets all work side by side today. everyone gets their own tile"
- Dolly, to us: "A tile is a box on the screen. One job per box."

**Panel 2.** Willy has leapt toward Dolly's tile and bumped the border between them. A small starburst sits on the line, and short motion lines trail behind him. His eyes are wide.
- Willy: "Ow! The edges are firm!"

**Panel 3.** No words. Dolly is now inside Willy's tile, right beside him. Willy stares with wide eyes, and Dolly's old tile is empty.

**Panel 4.** Both look calm, with heavy half-closed eyes.
- Willy: "How did you get in?"
- Dolly: "I asked."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
