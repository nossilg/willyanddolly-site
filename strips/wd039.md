# Halloween, strip 1 of 4: "Carve"

**Panel 1.** A big, plain pumpkin on the floor, with Willy on one side and Dolly on the other. Willy springs up, eager.
- John types: "carve a face in the pumpkin"
- Willy: "On it!"

**Panel 2.** No words. Daylight. The pumpkin is done: two small round holes, side by side, and nothing else. They're the same size and spacing as Willy's and Dolly's own eyes, and level with them. Pumpkin curls lie on the floor. Willy beams. Dolly nods: to her, it's a good face.

**Panel 3.** No words. Night. The pumpkin has gone into the dark; only its two holes glow.

**Panel 4.** Darker still. Three pairs of glowing dots in a row, all alike: Willy's, the pumpkin's, Dolly's.
- John types: "wait which one of you is the pumpkin"
- The two outer pairs turn to look at the middle pair. The middle pair keeps staring straight out.

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
