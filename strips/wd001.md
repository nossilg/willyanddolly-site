# Birthday Card

A split strip: the first panel, then the same job two ways, one row each.

**Panel 1, the start of both rows.** Willy and Dolly wait.
- John types: "need card 4 mom bday. she likes the bird thing not gerald. by 3"

## The first plan
**Panel 2.** Willy shoots out of the panel with speed lines behind him.
- Willy: "On it! Bird thing! Not Gerald! By 3!"
- Dolly: "...Who's Gerald?"

**Panel 3.** A big bird-shaped cake and a green parrot in a cage. Willy is proud.
- Willy: "Two birds. Neither one is Gerald. I checked."

**Panel 4.** The cake has slumped. The cage door is open and the parrot is leaving through the panel's edge.
- John types: "mom loves him. his name is gerald now too!"
- Willy: "...So it IS Gerald."

## The second plan
**Panel 2.**
- Dolly: "What's the bird thing?"
- John types: "the feeder by her window"
- Willy: "And Gerald?"
- John types: "her cat. hates birds"

**Panel 3.** Willy and Dolly make a card together: a cardinal at a feeder, with a grumpy gray cat glaring through a window. Inside, in Dolly's dots: "Happy birthday, Mom. Gerald sends nothing, as usual."

**Panel 4.** The card slides out past the edge. A clock reads 2:58.
- John types: "perfect. thank u"
- Willy: "We have two whole minutes."
- Dolly: "...What do people do with those?"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
