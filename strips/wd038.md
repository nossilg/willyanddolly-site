# Pass It On, strip 4 of 4: "We Had the Pieces"

**Panel 1.** Willy springs up, eager.
- John types: "helping my niece with a huge jigsaw. we lost the box lid"
- Willy: "Everyone, take a piece!"

**Panel 2.** Notes slide onto the board from every edge: "EDGES: done!", "RED: done!", then three "BLUE: sky?" and three "BLUE: sea?". The blue notes split the board down the middle.

**Panel 3.** Dolly turns to the edge.
- Dolly: "John, what was the picture?"
- John types: "a whale. its basically all whale"

**Panel 4.** Every blue note has flipped to "WHALE!". Willy beams.
- Willy: "We had the pieces. John had the picture."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
