# Pass It On, strip 2 of 4: "A Hundred Fives"

**Panel 1.** A note slides off Willy's tip and out the right edge. It says "5?".
- John types: "how many cups in a quart?"
- Willy: "Not sure. I'll ask!"

**Panel 2.** A board fills with notes from every edge. Every one says "5". Willy beams.
- Willy: "Everyone agrees!"

**Panel 3.** Dolly turns to the edge.
- Dolly: "John, can you check?"
- John types: "its 4. who said 5?"

**Panel 4.** Dolly has followed a trail of dots back from the board to the first note, the only one with a question mark: "5?".
- Willy, small: "I was asking."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
