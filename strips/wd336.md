# It's a Sentence

**Panel 1.** Two screens, a window on the left one. Four keys sit on the floor: super, shift, ctrl and an arrow. Willy and Dolly stand by them.
- John types: "say it with keys"

**Panel 2.** Willy presses one key at a time. Nothing moves.
- Willy: "SUPER!"
- Willy: "SHIFT!"

**Panel 3.** All four keys are held down together, and the window slides from the left screen to the right one.
- Dolly: "Super... Shift... Control... over there."

**Panel 4.**
- Willy: "IT'S A SENTENCE!"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
