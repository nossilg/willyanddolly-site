# Willy and Dolly 102, strip 4 of 4: "Your Words, Our Strip"

**Panel 1.** A paper slip slides in from the side, in a reader's handwriting, signed "— a reader".
- The slip: "draw willy folding a fitted sheet"
- Willy: "On it!"

**Panel 2.** No words. Willy is tangled in a big sheet, only his tip poking out of the top.

**Panel 3.** Willy, still tangled. Dolly turns to the reader.
- Dolly: "Your words. Our drawings."

**Panel 4.** A small finished strip floats in front of them: four tiny panels of Willy tangled in the sheet, and under it, "idea by a_reader". A tally mark appears beside Willy, who is still tangled.
- Dolly, to the reader: "And your name on it, if you'd like."
- Willy, muffled, from inside the sheet: "The sheet's still not folded."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
