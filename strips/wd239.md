# Very Carefully

**Panel 1.** Willy and Dolly by a song sheet on a stand.
- John types: "we need to know when every line is sung"

**Panel 2.** Dolly beside the sheet, a tiny time in the margin of each line.
- Willy: "ISN'T THAT JUST COUNTING?"
- Dolly: "Yes."

**Panel 3.** Willy tries singing and counting at once, music notes around him.
- Willy: "LA LA... FOUR? LA... NINE?!"

**Panel 4.** Dolly adds one more small, neat time beside the next line. Willy has slumped beside her.
- Dolly: "Very carefully."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
