# Letters Only, strip 2 of 2: "What Does It Take?"

The same request, a second way: this time Willy stops and asks.

**Panel 1.** The framed cake photo floats nearby. Willy has just started to launch, with skid marks at his base and a small "halt" mark by his tip.
- John types: "send mom the bday pic thru the slot thing"
- Willy: "On i— wait."

**Panel 2.** Willy is at the LETTERS ONLY slot. Dolly watches.
- Willy: "What does it take?"
- John types: "letters"

**Panel 3.** No words. Willy uses his bent tip like a pen to write the picture out on a page: "flame / candle / cake / pla…", one word per row.

**Panel 4.** Willy and Dolly side by side.
- John types: "she loved it. and u didnt even ask for my keys"
- Willy: "I wanted to."
- Dolly: "He really did."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
