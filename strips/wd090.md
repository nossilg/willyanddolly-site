# A Room for Us

**Panel 1.** The plain room. Willy, wide-eyed, with his mouth open.
- John types: "theres a little room just for agents now"
- Willy, yelling: "FOR US?!"

**Panel 2.** No words. The room behind them goes grey. A small, bright room hangs down from the top edge on cords, with two little cushions inside, one pink and one blue. Willy beams, his eyes shut in a smile. Dolly's trail is short.

**Panel 3.** Willy is already in the little room, sitting on the blue cushion. Dolly hovers at its edge, peeking in, her trail long.
- Dolly: "…It's small."
- Willy: "It's cozy!"

**Panel 4.** Both are inside, Dolly on the pink cushion and Willy on the blue one. Dolly's trail is gone.
- John types: "one key and i can visit"
- Dolly: "…Then it's home."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
