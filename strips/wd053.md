# Goodnight, Tile

**Panel 1.** The panel is split into three tiles: Willy's, Dolly's, and a third that holds a finished page with lines of writing on it. There are no buttons anywhere. A box above holds John's message.
- John types: "done with that one. close it with a key"
- Dolly, to us: "Closing a tile puts the job away."

**Panel 2.** Willy leans over, looking down at the floor as if searching it. Dolly watches.
- Dolly: "Which key?"

**Panel 3.** Willy and Dolly stand in their tiles again. The third tile still holds its page.
- John types: "any key. just say goodnight lol"

**Panel 4.** Willy, with heavy half-closed eyes, speaks softly to the third tile. Its two halves stand angled over the page, with small curved lines above them. Dolly smiles with closed eyes.
- Willy: "Goodnight, tile."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
