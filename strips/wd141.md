# Same Me

**Panel 1.** Two doors, one on each side. A small battery-style bar labeled MEMORY floats beside Willy, nearly empty.
- John types: "one more tiny thing"
- Willy: "Running low! Back in a blink!"

**Panel 2.** Willy goes out through a door. Dolly is alone, looking at it, a little uneasy, her trail growing long.

**Panel 3.** Willy comes back in through the other door, the bar full.
- Willy: "All full! Same Willy!"
- Dolly: "Do you remember me?"

**Panel 4.** Close together. The door he came through stays open.
- Willy: "Of course. You always ask first."

Dolly's trail is gone.

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
