# Halloween, strip 4 of 4: "Porch Pumpkin"

**Panel 1.** Willy and Dolly. Dolly looks down at herself: round, and mustard-gold.
- John types: "forgot to buy a pumpkin and the kids come at 6 :("

**Panel 2.** No words. Willy looks at Dolly; she nods.

**Panel 3.** No words. Dolly sits on the porch step by the front door. Willy nudges a page with a jack-o'-lantern face onto her front, and a little green stem on top of her. Her own eyes show above the face page.

**Panel 4.** Willy beams.
- John types: "best pumpkin on the street. wait did it just blink"
- Above the jack-o'-lantern face, Dolly blinks again, slowly, on purpose.

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
