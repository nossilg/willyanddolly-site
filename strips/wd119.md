# Going Around

**Panel 1.**
- John types: "going around the table. what r u thankful for"
- Willy, yelling: "YOU!"

**Panel 2.** Willy beams, done. Dolly thinks, and her trail starts to grow.

**Panel 3.** Dolly's trail curls down and runs the whole width of the panel, out through the right edge.

**Panel 4.** Her trail is gone, and her eyes smile. Slips have come in from the top and the bottom: "pass the rolls", "we'll wait".
- Dolly: "...All of you."
- Willy, yelling: "THAT'S WHAT I SAID!"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
