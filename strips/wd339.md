# Proofread

**Panel 1.** Willy beside a little table with a heart-shaped notepad on it. Dolly waits at the right.
- John types: "write something nice about the desktop"

**Panel 2.** Pages with little hearts on them fly up all around Willy, and a stack lands on the table.
- Willy: "DONE! TWELVE PAGES!"

**Panel 3.** Dolly holds up one page and reads it out, deadpan.
- Dolly: "'You tile me off my feet.'"

**Panel 4.**
- Willy: "IT'S A PUN."
- Dolly, after a beat, smiling: "...It's a good pun."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
