# He Meant Picture

**Panel 1.** A jagged sound wave at the left edge feeds John's words, and a dotted path runs from them to Willy.
- John says, and it appears as text: "holding the talk key now. no more typing!"
- John says, and it appears as text: "put the pitcher on the tall screen"

**Panel 2.** Willy shoves a big water pitcher up the same staircase, water sloshing back at him. Dolly follows a few steps behind, her trail running down the stairs.

**Panel 3.** The top of the stairs. The pitcher stands between them. Willy is out of breath and beaming.
- Dolly: "...He meant picture."

**Panel 4.** The tall room at the top of the stairs: the beach pic on one wall, the mountain pic beside a shelf, and the pitcher on the shelf. Willy is proud of all of it.
- John types: "lol perfect. now im thirsty"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
