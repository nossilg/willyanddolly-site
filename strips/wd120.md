# We Count

**Panel 1.** Willy sends a note out through the top, the right and the bottom of the panel: "How many for dinner?"
- John types: "how many for dinner? ask the house"
- Willy: "On it!"

**Panel 2.** Slips come back: 3 from the top, 2 from the right, 4 from the bottom. Nine tally marks stand beside Willy.
- Willy: "Upstairs, three! Down the hall, two! Downstairs, four!"

**Panel 3.** A tenth mark appears.
- Dolly: "Is that everyone?"
- John types: "thats 9. plus me. plus u two"

**Panel 4.** Twelve marks now: eleven beside Willy, and the last one, which he has nudged over, beside Dolly. Her eyes smile, and her trail is gone.
- Willy, softly: "We count."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
