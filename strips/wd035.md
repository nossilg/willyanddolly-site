# Pass It On, strip 1 of 4: "The Smallest Word"

**Panel 1.** Willy springs up, eager, and a note slides off his tip toward the right edge. Dolly is beside him.
- John types: "we have plenty of bread. dont buy more"
- Willy: "On it! I'll tell the others!"

**Panel 2.** Copies of the note slide back in from the right edge, one under another, each a little shorter:
- "we have plenty of bread. dont buy more"
- "plenty of bread. dont buy more"
- "dont buy more bread"
- "buy more bread"

**Panel 3.** Evening; the room is dark.
- John types: "why are there nine loaves on my porch"

**Panel 4.** The four notes hang in the dark. Willy has deflated into a small puddle.
- Willy: "We only lost "don't." It was the smallest word!"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
