# Letters Only, strip 1 of 2: "Every Key"

**Panel 1.** A framed photo of a birthday cake floats beside Willy, who leans forward, eager, with small motion lines at his base. Dolly is nearby.
- John types: "send mom the bday pic thru the slot thing"
- Willy: "On it!"

**Panel 2.** A mail slot is set into the right edge, under a little sign: LETTERS ONLY. The photo is jammed halfway in and bent. A ring of keys dangles from Willy's tip.
- Willy: "I'll need your house key. And the car. And whatever opens the shed."
- John types: "for a pic??"

**Panel 3.** Dolly drifts up to the slot. Behind her, the photo has been written out as a page of four big words, one per row, top to bottom like the picture: "flame / candle / cake / plate".
- Dolly: "What does it take?"
- John types: "letters"

**Panel 4.** Quiet. Willy droops, sheepish, the keys still dangling from his tip.
- John types: "she loved it. can i have my keys back"
- Willy: "I never even found out what the shed one opens."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
