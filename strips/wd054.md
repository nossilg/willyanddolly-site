# Neighbors

**Panel 1.** The panel is split into three tiles: a tall one at the left edge, and two stacked on the right. Willy is in the upper right tile, and Dolly is in the lower right tile. The tall tile is empty except for John's message.
- John types: "side by side today?"

**Panel 2.** Willy tilts and Dolly leans toward the tile line, both looking toward John's tile.
- Dolly: "That tile is where John types."

**Panel 3.** Willy stands in his tile. Dolly, with half-closed eyes, looks toward the left edge.
- Dolly: "He's been right next to us the whole time."

**Panel 4.** Willy beams, with light lines around him. Dolly smiles with closed eyes.
- Willy: "Hi, neighbor!"
- John types: "hi lol"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
