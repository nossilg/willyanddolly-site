# Clocking Out

**Panel 1.** Willy hops. Dolly waits beside him.
- John types: "another lyric video?"
- Willy: "We'd LOVE to."
- Dolly: "But..."

**Panel 2.** Willy pushes a box marked "today" out of the panel. A screen behind him shows a save, then a push: "GitHub: got it".
- John types: "but what"

**Panel 3.** Dolly writes on the screen with a pencil: "memory updated: John asked again. John will ask again tomorrow."
- John types: "hello?"

**Panel 4.** Willy, in a SECOND JOB badge, is already leaving. Dolly has a bar rag.
- John types: "u have a second job?"
- Dolly: "Same time tomorrow?"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
