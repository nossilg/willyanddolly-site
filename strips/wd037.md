# Pass It On, strip 3 of 4: "Small Note"

**Panel 1.** Willy springs up, eager.
- John types: "can someone make me lunch"
- Willy: "Everyone's on it!"

**Panel 2.** Notes pile onto the board, each bigger than the last: "skip step 2", "skip steps 2–9", "SKIP TO THE END!", "LUNCH FOR 500!". At the very bottom is one tiny note: "is this what John wanted?"

**Panel 3.** No words. Every note seems to wait. Dolly nudges the tiny note toward the edge.

**Panel 4.** Willy springs up again.
- John types: "no lol. just the one sandwich"
- Willy: "Back to step one!"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
