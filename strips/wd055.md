# So Sure, strip 1 of 5: "Misc 7"

**Panel 1.** Willy nudges a page with his tip onto a short pile labelled MISC. Dolly watches from the right.
- John types: "just put it in misc. future me will sort it"

**Panel 2.** More piles: MISC, MISC 2, MISC 3. Another page flies onto them. Willy beams, busy and happy. Dolly's trail of dots has grown.

**Panel 3.** No words. Two towers of pages now fill the panel, labelled MISC 4, MISC 5, MISC 6 and MISC 7. Willy and Dolly stand between them.

**Panel 4.** Willy and Dolly look at each other between the towers.
- John types: "future me here. what is misc 7"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow ring who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
