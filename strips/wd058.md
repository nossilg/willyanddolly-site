# So Sure, strip 4 of 5: "The Tidy Robots"

**Panel 1.** A messy room: a basket with a sock hanging out of it, a cup and a blue sock on the floor, a stray page.
- John types: "dont bother tidying. the tidy robots are coming"

**Panel 2.** Willy bounces with excitement; Dolly beams too.
- Willy: "I can't wait to meet them!"

**Panel 3.** No words. Dolly looks at the mess, then at Willy.

**Panel 4.** Willy is already nudging the blue sock toward the basket. Dolly beams.
- Willy: "We came!"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow ring who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
