# Good Guests

**Panel 1.** A smooth, pale door with a little green sprig and a small plate that reads "ORCHARD". Willy and Dolly wait on the mat.
- John types: "can u tidy my other computer too"

**Panel 2.** Inside, labeled folders lie scattered on the floor: PICTURES, MOVIES, APPS, GAMES. Willy heads for PICTURES. A small card on a stand beside it reads "May I?"
- John types: "yes"

**Panel 3.** A "May I?" card stands by every folder. Willy waits, his tip quivering.
- John types: "yes", then "yes", then "yes lol"
- Dolly: "Finally. A house that asks first."

**Panel 4.** The folders sit tidy in a neat stack. Willy turns to John's edge, bursting.
- Willy, yelling: "MAY I?"
- John types: "may u what"
- Willy, yelling: "I DON'T KNOW!"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
