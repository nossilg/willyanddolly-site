# Name Tags

**Panel 1.** The panel is split into three tiles: a tall one at the left edge, and two stacked on the right. In each tile stands a little hand-lettered sign on a stick: JOHN, WILLY and DOLLY. Willy beams, with light lines around him, and Dolly smiles with closed eyes.
- John types: "the whole point is no labels lol"

**Panel 2.** No words. The signs lie face-down on the floor. Willy leans over the one he has just turned over, with his eyes cast down. Dolly looks on.

**Panel 3.** Willy stands still in his tile.
- Dolly: "Then how will we know which tile is you?"
- John types: "im the one typing"

**Panel 4.** Willy beams again, with light lines around him.
- Willy: "Oh! That's the best label."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
