# Magic

**Panel 1.** Willy and Dolly stand at the foot of a staircase. A framed beach picture leans beside Willy, with a small label: "beach pic".
- John types: "put the beach pic on the tall screen"
- Dolly: "Which room is the tall one?"

**Panel 2.** Willy shoves the picture up the stairs with his tip, straining. Behind him, Dolly nudges the door at the foot of the stairs shut with her side.

**Panel 3.** A tall, narrow room with the beach picture on its wall. Willy and Dolly lie flattened on the floor beneath it, out of breath.
- John types: "magic!!"
- Dolly: "...Should we tell him?"
- Willy: "Let him have magic."

**Panel 4.** The view pulls way back. The whole staircase, forty steps, rises from a second framed picture at its foot, a mountain scene labeled "mountain pic". At the very top, tiny on the landing under the beach pic, Willy and Dolly look all the way down.
- John types: "do it again!!"

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
