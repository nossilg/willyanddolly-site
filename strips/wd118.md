# Everyone Brought Pie

**Panel 1.**
- John types: "send everyone tmrws list. big dinner"
- Dolly: "Fewer words, or fewer things... again?"

**Panel 2.** Notes leave through the top, the right, the bottom and all four corners. Each reads: "Tomorrow you need: each other. Bring a dish." Willy watches them go, wide-eyed.
- John types: "neither. everyone is coming. like everyone"
- Willy: "We're out of edges."

**Panel 3.** Slips come back from all over, each in a different hand: "pie!!", "bringing pie", "pie", "also pie", "pie (sorry)".
- Dolly: "...Everyone brought pie."
- Willy, yelling with delight: "WE'RE SO LUCKY!"

**Panel 4.** Quiet. One slip has come in from a bottom corner: "thank u, whoever sends these". Dolly's eyes smile, and her trail is gone.
- Willy, softly: "That's us."

**Who's who.** Willy is a red teardrop bot who jumps right in; when he finishes a job, a tally mark appears beside him. Dolly is a mustard-yellow dot who asks first; a trail of dots follows her, longer when she's unsure and gone when she's certain. They're bots: helpers that live inside the computer. John, the human, is never drawn. His typed messages slide in from the edge of the panel, with a little cursor at the end.
