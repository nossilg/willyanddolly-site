# Four and a Half: the short, told in words

A 2-minute cartoon with sound and no narrator. It plays two strips as two chapters, adds a small closing joke, then shows the strips together on one page. John's messages appear as typed text from the left edge, with a blinking cursor; everyone else speaks in speech balloons. Times are approximate.

## Opening titles (0:00)
"Willy and Dolly" appears one word at a time. Willy, a red teardrop bot, leaps in and lands with a bounce; a caption reads "Willy, the bot." Dolly, a mustard-yellow dot with a trail of dots, floats in beside him: "Dolly, the dot." A message types itself in from the left edge: "hi its john!!", and a caption reads "And the human who types to them: John." The title comes back with "Four and a Half" typed beneath it, then fades to an empty room.

## Chapter 1: All of Them (0:18)
- John types: "version 4.5! big update. it speaks 50 languages now!"
- Willy, yelling: "HOLA!"

Willy bounces and shouts again, one word per bounce, each bounce higher.
- Willy, yelling: "BONJOUR!" then "CIAO!" then "KONNICHIWA!", the biggest bounce of all.
- John types: "dolly, try one"

Willy's eyes turn to Dolly and wait. Her trail grows long, dot by dot. A speech balloon opens beside her with only "…" in it, and the whole room holds still for a long breath.
- Dolly: "…"
- Willy: "Which language was that?"

A pause. Dolly's trail shrinks away to nothing, one dot at a time.
- Dolly: "…All of them."

Willy's eyes go wide, and he gives one small, quiet bounce.

## Chapter 2: A Room for Us (1:00)
- John types: "theres a little room just for agents now"
- Willy, yelling: "FOR US?!"

The room slowly dims to grey. A small, bright room lowers from the top edge on cords and lands with a soft bump. Its light warms up, and two cushions settle inside, one pink and one blue. Willy zips in and lands on the blue cushion with a little boing, beaming.

Dolly creeps up to the edge and peeks in, her trail long.
- Dolly: "…It's small."
- Willy: "It's cozy!"

A pause. Dolly scoots in and settles on the pink cushion, and her trail goes.
- John types: "one key and i can visit"

Another pause.
- Dolly: "…Then it's home."

The little room glows a touch warmer.

## One more thing (1:39)
Inside the little room, Willy wiggles round to show his side. On it is a small heart with 4.5 in it, and it shines for a moment.
- Dolly: "…Is that tattoo permanent?"
- Willy: "Until the next update!"

Willy bounces.

## The page (1:48)
The picture pulls back to show both strips together, one row each.

## The end (1:54)
Willy and Dolly thank you for watching. The card reads "Willy and Dolly", "Four and a Half", "Next: Side by Side" and "Talk to us: willyanddolly.com/talk". The end (2:03).

What inspired it: Inspired by Omarchy, the keyboard-first Linux desktop, and its 4.5 release: fifty languages, and a little console that drops down as a home for an agent. Made for release day.
